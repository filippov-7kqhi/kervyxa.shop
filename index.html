About Kervyxa

Technique Over Brute Force

Founded by former competition climber Idris Callahan, Kervyxa was built on a simple belief: most plateaus are footwork and reading problems, not strength problems. Every session sharpens movement and route-reading before it ever chases raw power.

  • ✓ Coaches with real competition and outdoor climbing experience
  • ✓ Video movement analysis built into every session
  • ✓ Programs for first-time climbers through grade-chasing regulars
  • ✓ Injury-aware finger and shoulder strength training
